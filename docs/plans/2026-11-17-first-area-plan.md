slice.

## 1. Vision and core loop

**One line:** a bright, chunky Sproto fantasy valley where you fight with a staff up close and with
spells at range. Fights are short, readable and loud, and every encounter asks you to weave the two.

**Moment to moment (1–10 s):**
1. **Read** the wind-up: pose, eye glint, a ground decal, and for an unblockable attack a red flash
   plus its own shape and sound cue.
2. **Answer** it with a dodge, a block or parry, or by spacing.
3. **Punish** in the recovery with a light combo, a heavy attack or a spell.
4. **Weave.** Melee hits fill mana. A spell cast within 30 ticks of a melee hit is *woven*: it casts
   faster and deals more damage. Chilled or stunned enemies take extra melee damage. **Enemies have
   weaknesses and resistances** (§4.6). A Brute shrugs off spells and a Wisp shrugs off the staff,
   so a mixed camp forces you to switch tools.
5. **Manage stamina.** Attacks, dodges, blocks and sprinting share one bar.

**Five minutes:** shrine → marker or landmark → camp (2–6 enemies) → fight → loot → objective →
next shrine (rest, refill, camps respawn) → hand-in → gear or spell → out again.

**Session (60–90 min):** 5 main quests, 3 side quests, levels 1–7, **4 spells**, 3 shrines, and
Scrapjaw. A "Sproutvale cleared" card teases area 2. The game saves at shrines and at quest steps.

| Combat pillar | Rule |
|---|---|
| Hit-stop | Freezes the attacker and the victim: light 3 ticks, heavy 6, finisher 8, parry 10. A new hit-stop **takes the max** of the current and the new value, never the sum, **capped at 10**. Nothing ever freezes the whole world. |
| Buffer and cancels | §4.2: one buffer slot; combos at `cancel`, dodge from the end of the active frames. While an entity is in hit-stop, its buffer, combo and cancel clocks stop. |
| Dodge | Roll ≈ 34 ticks with i-frames on ticks 3–17. A **perfect dodge** (hit during i-frame ticks 3–9) refunds the stamina, gives +10 mana and makes the next hit a crit. No slow motion (co-op safe). |
| Block and parry | Hold block (staff guard). The first 10 ticks parry: melee attackers stagger, projectiles reflect (duel's Shield rule). |
| Lock-on | Soft target plus hard lock (§4.1). |
| Telegraphs | Normal wind-ups ≥ 18 ticks, heavy and AoE ≥ 30. AoE decals fill up. Unblockables get red, a jagged decal shape **and** a unique sound (readable without colour). |
| Economy | Stamina is short-term pacing. Mana is mostly earned by hitting. |
| Poise | Breaking poise causes a stagger. Bosses become **Broken**. |
| Group pressure | Attack tokens (§4.8). |
| One difficulty, varied enemies (owner) | No difficulty modes. Challenge comes from enemy roles (melee, ranged, caster), weaknesses and resistances, and from bosses with far more HP and far harder hits than normal enemies. |
| Feedback | Hit sounds, staff whooshes and pad rumble (light, heavy and parry profiles) ship in **M1**, not in polish **(main session's call)**. |

## 2. Architecture

### 2.1 Folder and module layout

```
src/
  main.ts            title shell; dynamic-imports app/
  app/               loop.ts (fixed step), app state machine, error/veil panels (§2.9)
  sim/               PURE simulation (no three, DOM, clock or Math.random)
    SimWorld.ts  entity.ts  commands.ts  events.ts  rng.ts  hash.ts  view.ts (ReadonlySimView)
    physics/         ONLY Rapier importer: PhysicsWorld, groups, queries, cell bodies
    systems/  combat/  ai/  quests/  dialogue/  progression/
  content/           PURE typed data
    moves.ts  spells.ts  statuses.ts  enemies.ts  bosses/scrapjaw.ts  items.ts  loot.ts
    quests.ts  dialogue/*.ts  npcs.ts  progression.ts  tuning.ts
    generated/       playerMoveTiming.ts, playerHitboxes.ts (written by tools, never by hand)
  world/             RegionManifest, CellStreamer, TerrainChunk, PropInstancer, Horizon
  render/            Renderer, views/, anim/ (Animator, ProceduralMotion), fx/
  assets/  input/  camera/  audio/  ui/  save/  debug/  workers/terrain.worker.ts
tools/               bake-world.mjs, gen-player-moves.mjs, import-sibling-assets.mjs, optimize-assets.mjs
scripts/             check-assets, check-budgets, load-profile, check-prod-debug
art/                 (exists) Blender sources and melee_anim.py
world-src/sproutvale/  terrain.json, placements.json
public/assets/...  public/world/sproutvale/{region.json, cells/*, horizon.glb}
tests/               architecture, sim, content, golden, balance, e2e
```

Dependency direction: `content` ← `sim` ← everything else. `render` and `ui` get a
`ReadonlySimView` (state plus read-only queries: `raycast`, `sphereCast`) and send commands. They
never mutate sim state.

### 2.2 The simulation contract

- `SimWorld.tick(commands: ReadonlyMap<PlayerId, Command>)` advances one 60 Hz tick. The sim has no
  `localPlayer`.
- **Commands carry intents, not effects (main session's call).** The command kinds are:
  `input{move, camYaw, buttons}`, `interact{id}`, `talk{npc}`, `dialogueChoose{choice}`,
  `buy{merchant, item}`, `equip{item}`, `usePotion`, `spendAttr{attr}`, `rest`. The sim owns each
  player's dialogue cursor and evaluates the conditions and effects itself. A client never sends
  "give item X".
- **Floats, not fixed point (architect's call).** Co-op is planned as host-authoritative: snapshot
  interpolation for remote entities and prediction for your own character. Determinism is only
  needed within one build (for tests and replays). If lockstep is ever needed, switch to
  `rapier3d-deterministic-compat` and review the maths.
- **`hash()` and `snapshot()` cover sim entity state only**: entities, rng streams, quests and
  flags. They do **not** cover Rapier's internals. Restoring a snapshot rebuilds the Rapier world
  from entity bodies plus the loaded cells.
- Randomness only comes from `world.rng.<stream>`, seeded by world seed + stream name.

### 2.3 The fixed-timestep loop

This is the shooter loop: `DT = 1/60`, a frame clamp of 0.25 s, at most 5 ticks per frame, and
interpolation with `alpha`. Hit-stop is per entity (`entity.hitstop`, combined as max with a cap of
10). While it is above 0, that entity's move clock, buffer, cancel windows and velocity are frozen,
and the renderer freezes its animation. The camera uses live yaw and pitch. Each command carries
`camYaw`.

System order: `applyCellQueue(colliders) → commands(intents) → playerIntent → aiPerception →
aiDecide(tokens) → moveRunner → locomotion(KCC) → physics.step → hitboxResolve → damage/poise/status
→ projectiles → spells → statusTick → vitals → interactables/dialogue → triggers → spawners → quests
→ progression → cleanup`.

### 2.4 The entity model: plain data with optional components, plus systems (architect's call)

```ts
interface Entity {
  id: EntityId; kind: 'player'|'enemy'|'boss'|'npc'|'projectile'|'area'|'pickup'|'interactable';
  tf: { pos: Vec3; prevPos: Vec3; yaw: number; vel: Vec3 };
  body?: { radius: number; height: number };            // Rapier handle kept in physics/, keyed by id
  health?; vitals?; stats?; inventory?; questLog?; dialogue?: { npc: NpcId; node: NodeId };
  combat?: { team; poise; poiseMax; move: ActiveMove|null; buffer: Buffered|null; iframes; parry;
             blocking; hitstop; hitstun; lastHitTick; softTarget: EntityId|null; lock: EntityId|null };
  statuses?: StatusInstance[]; ai?: AiState; owner?: PlayerId; view: { model: ModelKey; tint?: number };
}
```

There are 30–60 live entities. A full ECS library hurts how readable and serializable the data is
and buys nothing at this scale. Class hierarchies make snapshots and co-op deltas harder. Plain
objects can be cloned and hashed and match the shooter's sim. Systems query cached arrays.

### 2.5 Data-driven content and generated timing

- Everything is typed `const` data with `satisfies` checks, cross-reference tests and a pinned
  **content fingerprint**. Tuning hot-reloads through Vite HMR.
- **`npm run gen:moves`** (`tools/gen-player-moves.mjs`) reads `player.melee.json` and `player.glb`
  and writes:
  - `content/generated/playerMoveTiming.ts`: every marker as `round(sec × 60)` ticks.
  - `content/generated/playerHitboxes.ts`: for each move and each active tick, a capsule from the
    staff butt to the staff tip in character space. The tool loads the GLB through gltf-transform,
    drops textures, and samples the clip at 60 Hz with three's `AnimationMixer` in Node. Butt and tip
    come from `socket_staff`'s `extras.bindPoseWorld` (`butt`, `top`). Capsule radius 0.18 m.
  - `gen:moves -- --check` fails if either generated file is stale (it is in the checks).

  `content/moves.ts` holds only what is designed by hand (damage, poise, stamina, hitstun,
  pushback, lunge) and an optional per-move `rate`.

### 2.6 Event bus

The sim pushes typed `SimEvent`s each tick (`hit`, `dodgePerfect`, `parry`, `stagger`,
`statusApplied`, `death`, `spellCast`, `telegraph{shape, unblockable, ticks}`, `itemGained`,
`questUpdated`, `levelUp`, `bossPhase`, `shrineRest`, …). The app re-emits them for audio, FX,
feel, rumble, HUD and camera. Inside the sim, quests and progression read the same tick's event array
in their fixed slot.

### 2.7 State machines

Character state comes from the move runner (`locomotion | move | dodge | block | hitstun | stagger
| knockdown | stunned | dead`), with transitions only through data. Enemies use a small HFSM (§4.8).
Bosses use phase tables. App states: `Boot → Title → Loading → Playing ⇄ Paused/Menu/Dialogue →
Credits`, plus `Error`.

### 2.8 Saving (main session's call: one slot)

- `localStorage`: `sow.save` and `sow.save.bak` (the previous good write), plus `sow.settings` and
  `sow.controls`. The save is JSON of about 20 KB or less.
- `SaveFile { schema; savedAt; build; player{level, xp, attrs, potions, equipment, inventory, spells,
  slots}; quests; flags; world{lastShrine, openedChests, killedUniques, discoveredShrines} }`.
  Enemy positions are never saved.
- `migrations.ts` runs `v → v+1` steps, with a fixture save and a test for each version. A parse or
  migration failure falls back to `.bak` and says so on screen.
- Autosave at a shrine rest, a quest step, the boss's death, and every 5 minutes out of combat.
  `savedAt` comes from the app, not the sim.

### 2.9 Unhandled cases (must be handled; each has a test or a QA script step)

| Case | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Window loses focus, or pointer lock is lost | Auto-pause and open the pause menu. A pad user is not forced back into pointer lock. |
| WebGL context loss | Pause and show a "Restoring graphics…" veil. On `webglcontextrestored`, re-upload templates and rebuild cell meshes from cached data. After 10 s, offer "Reload (progress saved at last autosave)". |
| Rapier init fails | Show an error panel with the reason and a Retry button. The game never starts half-initialized. |
| Cell fetch fails | 3 retries with backoff (0.5, 1, 2 s). The player's cell and its neighbours sit behind a loading veil (the player is frozen). After a **15 s veil timeout**, show "Retry / Return to last shrine". |
| Death in the M1 sandbox | Respawn at the sandbox spawn after 2 s with full resources. The current wave restarts. |
| Death in the world | Respawn at the last shrine with potions refilled. Camps respawn. A boss fight resets fully. |

## 3. World streaming

### 3.1 How the world is divided

- World → regions → **64 m cells** keyed by global `(cx, cz)`. **Sproutvale is 8 × 8 cells, 512 m ×
  512 m (architect's call).**
- Each cell has `c_X_Z.bin` (65×65 `Uint16` heights at 1 m spacing; 65×65 RGBA8 splat weights) and
  `c_X_Z.json` (prop placements grouped by key as packed arrays; landmarks; spawners; interactables;
  NPC spawns; trigger volumes).
- `region.json`: bounds, the cell list with sizes, and the prop table
  (`key → {path, lod1Path, collider, landmark, **capacity**}`). `capacity` is the **baked maximum
  number of instances of that prop in any 7×7 cell window** (the hysteresis ring; was 5×5 before M2), so instanced meshes are sized once and
  never resized. It also lists shrines, the player start and the horizon mesh.
- Each cell is **≤ 120 KB**. Prop GLBs load once per region.

### 3.2 What loads when

The streamer takes a list of **anchors**: the player (one now, N in co-op), **plus any enemy that is
in combat or leashing home**. Enemy anchors only keep the colliders of their own cell (3×3 for the
player), so a fight or a leash never runs onto unloaded ground **(main session's call)**.

| Ring | Chebyshev distance | Loaded |
|---|---|---|
| Active | ≤ 1 | Full-res terrain, heightfield and prop colliders, spawners and enemies, NPCs, interactables |
| Visual | ≤ 2 | 2 m terrain, LOD1 props, no colliders or entities |
| Hysteresis | 3 | What's loaded stays, nothing new loads |
| Unload | ≥ 4 | Dropped |
| Horizon | whole region | `horizon.glb` (8 m grid, about 8k triangles, plus landmark impostors), always resident |

- **Fog runs linearly from 80 to 180 m** and the far plane is 450 m. The visual ring stays at 2
  (it reaches at least 128 m), so fog hides the edge of the LOD1 band, and only the horizon mesh and
  landmarks show past 180 m.
- **The horizon mesh is drawn 0.5 m lower and masked out under loaded cells** (a 64×64 mask texture
  of loaded cells, updated when the ring changes, and fragment discard), so it never z-fights or
  pokes through real terrain.
- Prefetch: cells along velocity × 4 s load at LATER priority.
- **Colliders:** each cell has **one fixed rigid body with one compound collider set**: the
  heightfield plus primitive prop colliders. Cell bodies are created at the top of a tick through
  `applyCellQueue`, with at most **96 colliders created per tick** (a big cell spreads over a few
  ticks; the player's own cell and its neighbours are forced first). Unloading removes the whole
  body.
- Entities: spawners live only in Active cells. Idle enemies despawn when their cell deactivates,
  and the killed list lasts until the next rest. Enemies in combat or leashing stay, and anchor
  their cell. Uniques and the boss are controlled by world flags.
- The player is never left without ground: `Loading → Playing` needs the colliders of the player's
  cell and its neighbours, otherwise the veil from §2.9 shows.

### 3.3 Loading and decode

GLTFLoader with `MeshoptDecoder`, using the shooter's NOW/LATER queue (4 requests in parallel).
WebP textures inside the GLB, with edge caps: player 2048 px, other characters 1024, props 512,
terrain layers 1024. **No KTX2 in the slice**, following the shooter's measurement. It is re-evaluated
for terrain only in M6 if memory goes over budget. `terrain.worker.ts` builds the terrain geometry
and the heightfield arrays. The main thread uploads at most 2 textures and 4 geometries per frame.
Template refcounts are per cell, and a template with no refs is disposed after 10 s.

### 3.4 Instancing, LOD and shadows

- **One `InstancedMesh` per (prop, LOD) across all cells**, sized to the baked `capacity`. Cells
  swap-remove their instance ranges. Draw calls scale with prop kinds (about 30), not with cell count.
- LOD0 within 70 m, LOD1 (gltf-transform `simplify` to 30%) from 70 to 180 m. Beyond that only
  landmarks show, through the horizon set. Instances are re-bucketed every 15 ticks, per cell.
- Terrain: 64×64 quads in Active cells, 32×32 in Visual cells, with skirts and a 4-layer splat.
- Skinned characters: full rate within 30 m, 30 Hz from 30 to 60 m, 10 Hz beyond.
- Shadows: one 2048² directional map that follows the player and covers about 70 m. **The shadow
  pass counts toward the draw-call budget**: shadow casters are LOD0 props and characters within
  60 m only.
- Grass is **deferred until after M5** (§9).

### 3.5 Budgets

Target: a mid-range laptop (Iris Xe or M1 base, 1080p, Chrome).

|---|---|---|

### 3.6 How the area is authored (architect's call)

`world-src/sproutvale/terrain.json` holds seeded fBm noise plus stamps: `flatten` (village, arena),
`raise` (hills), `carve` (river, ravine), `bowl` (quarry) and `path` splines (painted into the path
splat layer and slightly flattened). `placements.json` holds hand placements plus scatter rules
(density, slope and height limits, exclusion masks, seeded). `npm run bake:world -- sproutvale` is
deterministic: the same sources give the same bytes, which a test checks. It computes instance
capacities and the horizon mesh. Before M5, placements are edited by hand in JSON with a debug
"copy position" hotkey. The in-game placement editor is deferred. **A second area** is a new
`world-src/<region>` placed at other global cells, connected by an edge (border cells load from both
manifests) or a portal trigger. The streamer works on global cells plus the manifests near an
anchor, and nothing about Sproutvale is hard-coded elsewhere.

## 4. Combat design in detail (ticks at 60 Hz)

### 4.1 Controls, camera and lock-on

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Pad (Xbox) |
|---|---|---|
| Move, sprint, jump | WASD, Shift, Space | LS, LS-click, A |
| Light / heavy (hold to charge) | LMB / RMB | RB / RT |
| Dodge roll | C | B |

All of it is remappable through duel's `controlPrefs`. **The gamepad is first-class from M0

- **Camera collision (M1):** a sphere cast of radius 0.25 m from the shoulder pivot to the desired
  camera position, through `ReadonlySimView.sphereCast` (Rapier stays inside the sim). The camera
  snaps in when blocked and eases back out at 4 m/s. Characters between the camera and the player
  fade, as in duel.
- **Soft target:** at attack or spell startup, the best enemy within 4 m (melee) or 25 m (spells)
  in a 60° cone of the input direction. The player turns toward it at up to 720°/s during startup.
- **Hard lock:**
  - **Acquire:** score enemies within 20 m in a 70° cone of the camera forward that have line of
    sight; the lowest `angle° + 0.5·distance_m` wins.
  - **Camera:** the pivot sits 70% of the way toward the player. Yaw eases toward facing the target,
    pitch is clamped to −35° to +20°, and the camera pulls back by up to 2 m for targets taller than
    3 m. Bosses have a `lockPoint` in their `EnemyDef`.
  - **Switch:** a mouse flick over 300 px/s horizontal, or the right stick over 0.7 (then re-armed
    under 0.3). Picks the nearest enemy in that screen direction.
  - **Break:** beyond 28 m, line of sight lost for more than 1 s, or the target dies. On a death,
    lock passes to the nearest enemy within 10 m, otherwise it releases.
  - **Movement:** while locked, the player strafes around the target (the existing strafe clips).

- **Scrubbing:** the Animator maps sim ticks to clip time **piecewise-linearly between the markers
  `start, activeStart, activeEnd, cancel, end`**, with an optional per-move uniform `rate` (default
  1, which is the identity). Faster startups are a tuning option: set `rate`, or retime the clip
  in `melee_anim.py` and re-run the art pipeline. Because each light clip starts from the previous
  one's follow-through pose, **combos open at `cancel`** and L2 starts with no pop. Combo heavy and
  riposte enter `attack_heavy` at its charge start (18) with a 4-tick crossfade.
- **Charge:** while heavy is held, clip time stays inside the json's charge window [18, 29), either
  frozen at 29 or ping-ponging there (tuning flag). The charged level is reached after 30 held
  ticks, and at most 60 ticks can be held. Releasing resumes from 29.
- **Buffer rule (C2):**
  - **One slot. The newest press replaces an older one**, except that dodge is never replaced by an
    attack in the same tick.
  - **An attack or spell pressed after the current move's `activeStart` is held until that move's
    `cancel` opens**, however long that is (L1: a press on tick 9 fires on tick 20). Otherwise a
    buffered press lives **10 ticks**, and a dodge press lives 6 ticks.
  - It is consumed on the **first tick the action is legal**: an attack at `cancel` or later; a dodge
    at `activeEnd` or later, or any time in locomotion or block.
  - Presses during hitstun or stagger are dropped, not buffered.
  - **The buffer, combo and cancel clocks do not advance while the entity is in hit-stop.**

  M1's tests prove a buffered press fires exactly on the first legal tick, including a light
  pressed on every tick of the previous light's active and recovery frames.
- **Hitstun numbers (QA C3):** lights 20 ticks, so L1 → L2 → L3 is a true combo on a non-elite;
  heavy 28; L3 knockdown 45. Hyper armor ignores hitstun. **The player** takes 18 ticks of hitstun
  from a normal enemy hit (bosses' heavy hits use their own stagger values).
- **Combo reset:** with no follow-up by `end`, the player returns to locomotion and the string
  resets. Hyper armor on heavies runs from charge start (18, or 0 for the combo heavy), so light
  hits don't interrupt them but damage still applies.
- **Lunge (main session's call):** during startup, the player moves `clamp(dist − reach, 0, 1.5 m)`
  toward the soft target (`reach` = the move's generated hitbox reach, about 1.6 m). With no
  target, the move uses 0.4 m. The motion comes from the sim (root motion is pinned).
- **Hitboxes:** the generated staff capsules, one per active tick, run through Rapier
  `intersectionsWithShape` against `HURTBOX`, plus a line-of-sight ray against `WORLD`. Each target
  is hit at most once per move. Hurtboxes are character capsules. Debug view: red hitboxes, green
  hurtboxes.
- **Hitstun** applies to non-elite enemies whose poise didn't absorb the hit. Elites and bosses take
  only damage and poise until they break. Pushback is applied over 6 ticks through the KCC.
- **Plunge** (an attack in the air) is **deferred until after M5**.

### 4.3 Dodge, block and parry

- **Roll:** ≈ 34 ticks over 5 m, with i-frames on ticks 3–17 and a cost of 22 stamina. It can
  cancel into a roll attack (L1 data, MV 1.2) from tick 26. Duel's `roll` clip is time-scrubbed to
  34 ticks. Pressing dodge with 0 stamina does nothing and flashes the bar. **Backstep is deferred**.
- **Block** (`block` clip: raised at tick 7 (`raise_done` 0.12 s), held from `hold_from`, lowered
  to `end` at 30): blocks a 150° frontal arc. A blocked hit deals 20% chip and costs stamina equal to
  poise ÷ 2. Guard break: 40-tick stagger. Unblockables ignore block.
- **Parry:** ticks 0–9 of the press. Against melee: the attacker staggers for 60 ticks (a boss loses
  35% of its poise), the riposte window opens, the player gains +15 mana, and both get 10 ticks of
  hit-stop. Against a projectile: reflected at 1.5× speed and damage. A missed parry simply
  becomes a block. A block raised within 20 ticks of the last one gets only a 4-tick parry window.

### 4.6 The damage formula

```
power  = melee: staff.damage * (1 + 0.04*Might)      spell: spell.base * staff.spellPower * (1 + 0.04*Arcana)
mitig  = power * mv * 100 / (100 + target.defense[physical|arcane])
mult   = (crit ? 1.5 : 1)          // riposte, backstab ≤ 60° behind, perfect-dodge follow-up; never random
       * (stunned|frozen ? 1.25 : 1) * (woven ? 1.2 : 1) * clamp(1 + 0.05*(attLv - defLv), 0.8, 1.2)
       * (melee on Chilled ? 1.3, consumes Chill : 1)
       * target.profile.damage[kind]          // weak 1.5 | normal 1 | resist 0.5  (owner)
damage = max(1, round(mitig * mult));  poise -= poiseDmg * (crit ? 1.5 : 1)
```

There is no random variance and there are no random crits **(architect's call)**.

**Weakness and resistance model (owner's answer 3).**
- Every `EnemyDef` has `profile: { damage: { physical, arcane }, status: { burn, chill, shock } }`,
  each value `'weak' | 'normal' | 'resist'`.
- **Damage:** ×1.5, ×1 or ×0.5.
- **Statuses:** a weakness multiplies duration and potency by 1.5 (Burn damage per second, Chill
  slow, Shock poise penalty), and a single Frost Nova adds 3 Chill stacks instead of 2. A resistance
  multiplies by 0.5. **Nothing is immune in area 1**, so the "wrong" tool still works, only slower.
  A content test enforces this.
- **Reflected projectiles** keep their original kind: a reflected hex is arcane, a reflected coin
  is physical.
- **Poise damage** is multiplied the same way as damage, so the right tool also breaks guards faster.
- **The player sees it:**
  - The first hit of each kind on an enemy type reveals that kind's icon on its health bar (a sword
    or a rune, plus status icons), and the reveal is remembered for the session.
  - Damage numbers come in a **"Weak!"** style (larger, orange, with a punch-in) and a **"Resisted"**
    style (smaller, grey, with a dull sound).
  - The first time each happens, a toast explains it.

### 4.8 Enemy AI

- **HFSM:** `Idle/Patrol → Alerted (0.6 s, alerts its camp) → Engage{Approach | Circle |
  WaitForToken | Attack | Recover} → Reacting{Hitstun | Stagger | Knockdown | Frozen} → Leash (2×
  speed, heals, ignores the player; an anchor) → Dead`.
- **Perception:** a 120° cone of 22 m, with a line-of-sight ray every 6 ticks (staggered). Hearing
  of 12 m on `hit` and `spellCast`.
- **Attack choice:** weighted by distance band and cooldowns (rng stream `ai`). No move more than
  twice in a row.
- **Tokens (per player):** 2 melee and 1 ranged. A Brute's heavy costs 2. A token is taken at
  wind-up and released at the end of recovery or on an interrupt. Enemies without one circle at
  4–7 m and feint. Bosses are exempt, and their adds share the pool. Threat picks the target in co-op.
- **Navigation:** steering, separation and 3 whisker rays on the KCC. No navmesh. Enemies stuck for
  more than 5 s teleport home. `recast-navigation-js` is the escape hatch **(architect's call)**.
- **Enemy motion is placeholder:** native clips where they exist, otherwise `cast` and `cast_big`
  scrubbed to the frame data, plus `ProceduralMotion.ts` additive poses (wind-up lean, glint shake,
  leap arc). Enemy hitboxes are shapes defined by hand in `EnemyDef` moves.

## 5. RPG systems (sized for the slice)

- **Attributes:** Might, Arcana, Vitality, Endurance. Fixed growth each level plus **1 point** to
  spend at a shrine. Level cap 10.
- **XP curve `round(60·L^1.45)` (main session's call):** 60, 164, 295, 448, 620, 807, 1,008… The
  cumulative XP to reach L6 is **1,587** and to reach L7 is **2,394**. QA's totals were about 1,850
  XP for the main path before the boss, and about 2,650 for a full clear. Those mean **level 6 on
  the main path and level 7 with the side content** when the player reaches Scrapjaw, which is the
  intended range. The old curve (`80·L^1.6`, L6 = 2,573) left main-path players at level 5. Enemy
  XP: Grunt 12, Slinger 18, Brute 60, Gatekeeper 300, Scrapjaw 1,200. Re-check the totals with the
  balance bot's kill log in M4.
- **Gear:** Staff (§4.6), Robe (4 colour tints, physical and arcane defense, one small modifier),
  Charm (6 data-only: Quick Step, Ember Heart, Second Wind, Scholar's Knot, Iron Root, Spark
  Catcher). Rarities are Common, Rare and Epic, each with fixed stats. No procedural affixes
  **(architect's call)**.
- **Drops** (rng `loot`): Sprouts currency (`gem.glb`) always; hearts 15%; then items from short
  lists. Chest contents are fixed. **The merchant** sells Nobu, two charms and +1 Tonic charge, all
  through `buy{merchant, item}`.
- **Inventory:** lists per category, no weight, capped at 40 items.
- **Quests:**

```ts
type Objective = { kind:'kill'; tag; count } | { kind:'collect'; item; count; consume? } | { kind:'talk'; npc; node? }
               | { kind:'reach'; trigger } | { kind:'interact'; object };
type Effect = { give } | { xp } | { learnSpell } | { setFlag } | { startQuest } | { spawnUnique } | { openGate } | { currency };
interface QuestDef { id; title; giver; kind:'main'|'side'; prereq?; steps: { id; text; objectives; marker?; onComplete: Effect[] }[]; rewards }
type QuestState = { status:'locked'|'available'|'active'|'complete'; step; progress: number[] };
```

  The quest runtime is a sim system that matches each tick's events. Every `reach` trigger sits
  where the player must stand, so its cell is always loaded. The quest log (J) shows step text,
  progress and the tracked quest. The tracked objective drives a **compass-bar marker** (the map
  menu is deferred).
- **Dialogue:** `DialogueGraph { nodes: { speaker; text; choices?: { text; next?; if?: Condition[];
  do?: Effect[] }[]; next?; do? } }`, with NPC entry rules ordered by condition. **The sim owns the
  cursor.** The UI shows `player.dialogue.node` and sends `dialogueChoose{choice}`, and the sim
  checks conditions and applies effects. The panel shows name and text (typewriter) and the camera
  frames the speaker. Render-to-texture portraits are deferred.
- **UI: DOM overlay (architect's call).** Dirty-checked writes. No layout reads in the frame loop.
  - **HUD:** HP, stamina and mana bars; XP; spell bar with cooldowns; tonic count; quest tracker;
    compass; lock reticle; boss bar; prompts; toasts; damage numbers.
  - **In the canvas:** enemy bars (with revealed weakness and resistance icons), status icons and
    telegraph decals.
  - **Menus:** Pause, Inventory/Character, Quest log, Settings (duel's controller settings,
    graphics, motion reduction for shake and flash), Shrine (Rest, Level up).

### 7.3 Import and optimization pipeline

- `tools/import-sibling-assets.mjs` copies the listed files, **gunzips `*.glb.gz`**, re-fits
  staffs, decimates any standard enemy over 9k triangles, transcodes WAV to **Opus** (`.webm`, with
  an `.m4a` fallback for older Safari), and writes `assets/IMPORTED.json` (source repo, path, commit,
  sha256). The copies are committed, and nothing references the sibling repos at runtime.
- `scripts/optimize-assets.mjs` (port of the shooter's) runs meshopt, quantize, WebP, edge caps,
  `simplify` for LOD1, prune and dedup. **It skips `characters/player.glb`**, because art/README.md
  warns that `gltf-transform optimize` strips the empty `socket_staff` node. The player is
  compressed only by the art pipeline's resample → webp → meshopt steps.
- `check-assets` checks triangle caps by tier and clip lists. It also checks that `player.glb` has
  `socket_staff` and that `gen:moves -- --check` passes.
- **Provenance and licensing:** every sibling asset is the owner's own (through the owner's
  Higgsfield, Tripo and Meshy accounts, or made in-house). The player's melee clips come from
  `art/tools/melee_anim.py` + `art/source/player_melee.blend` → `player.glb` + `player.melee.json`
  (art/README.md). `IMPORTED.json` records every copied file. **Likeness:** the Obama and Pepe
  models may ship publicly, and the first public release is a test release (owner's answer 4).

### 7.4 The later art path

## 8. Testing and QA

- **Unit and sim (Vitest, Node, Rapier init in setup):**
  - Move runner: generated timing equals the json converted to ticks; combo at `cancel`; dodge at
    `activeEnd`.
  - Buffer rule: first legal tick, replacement, dropped in hitstun, frozen in hit-stop.
  - Hit-stop combines as max with a cap of 10.
  - I-frame and perfect-dodge windows; parry windows and the spam penalty.
  - Damage table cases and the §4.10 fight maths.
  - Weakness and resistance multipliers on damage, poise and status duration and potency; a
    reflected projectile keeps its kind.
  - Poise; statuses; tokens (≤ 2 melee wind-ups over 10k ticks); leash; quest matching.
  - Dialogue: commands with a forbidden choice are rejected.
  - Loot distribution; the XP totals at quest checkpoints.
- **Content tests:**
  - Cross-references, wind-ups ≥ 18, the fingerprint.
  - **Every `EnemyDef` has a full profile.** No `immune` value exists in area 1.
  - Across the four archetypes there is at least one physical-resist and one arcane-resist type, and
    each status is a weakness somewhere.
- **Architecture test:**
  - `src/sim` and `src/content` never use `three`, the DOM, `Math.random`, `Date.now`,
    `performance.now`, `localStorage` or timers.
  - Rapier is imported only under `src/sim/physics`.
  - The sim never imports `render`, `ui`, `world`, `app`, `audio` or `input`.
  - `render` and `ui` only see `ReadonlySimView`.
  - The loader keeps meshopt, 4 parallel requests and per-cell requests.
- **Goldens:** scripted command streams → the hash at fixed ticks (`npm run gen:goldens`, regenerated
  on purpose).
- **Balance bots (main session's call): `npm run test:balance`, not part of `npm test`, 10 seeds.**
  Run it in M4 and M5 and on any PR that touches `content/`. **The bot reads the target's profile
  and picks the right damage kind** (melee against phys-weak or arc-resist targets, spells against
  phys-resist ones, Frost Nova on chill-weak Brutes). A paired check proves the profiles matter: a
  melee-only bot takes ≥ 1.8× longer on a Wisp camp than the profile-aware bot.
  - A level-6 bot with starter gear beats Scrapjaw in ≥ 7 of 10 seeds, in 3–8 min of sim time.
  - A bot that never dodges loses within 90 s.
  - A level-2 bot dies to a 5-Grunt camp in ≤ 3 of 10 seeds.
- **Budgets:** `check:assets`, `budgets`, `profile` / `profile:check`, `bake:world -- --check`,
  `gen:moves -- --check`.
- **Playwright smoke** (`tests/e2e/smoke.spec.ts`, `?seed=1&qa=1`):
  1. Boot to playable with no console errors.
  2. `window.__qa` teleports through a 4-cell path. Assert the **streamer counters** (loaded,
     unloaded, queued, colliders created) and **zero `longtask` entries over 50 ms**.
  3. Read `renderer.info` at 4 spots.
  4. Kill a dummy.
  5. Save → reload → load gives the same state hash.
  6. Simulate a context loss through `WEBGL_lose_context`, and confirm it recovers.

  **Frame time is not asserted in CI.** It is measured by hand on the target laptop and recorded in
  milestone PRs.
- **Debug overlay** (backtick key):
  - **Perf:** FPS, frame and sim ms, draw calls (main and shadow), triangles, heap, cell rings,
    loader queue.
  - **Combat:** hitboxes and hurtboxes, the **frame-data timeline** (markers, buffer, i-frames and
    parry window, hit-stop), AI state and tokens.
  - **Cheats:** time scale, single-step, god mode, infinite resources, teleport, spawn menu, set
    level, give item, complete step, and a "copy position" hotkey.

## 10. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | L / I | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Combat isn't fun | Med / **High** | Two fun gates (M1a, M1b); tuning in data with hot reload; the timeline view; clips retimed through the script |
| The generated clip timing feels slow (heavy startup 31 ticks, L3 18) | Med / Med | A per-move `rate`; piecewise scrubbing; retime `TIMING` in `melee_anim.py` and re-run (owner's answer 5) |
| Placeholder enemy motion | High / Med | Native clips for the Brute, Gatekeeper and boss; procedural additive poses; telegraph FX and sound carry readability |
| Streaming hitches | Med / High | Terrain worker, upload caps, collider cap per tick, compound bodies, baked capacities, a long-task gate |
| Rapier WASM size and init | Med / Med | Measured in M0, parallel init, budgeted, an error panel on failure |
| Skinned cost in fights | Med / Med | Animation rate by distance; ≤ 4 elite-tier characters active; tokens |
| No navmesh | Med / Med | Clearings, whiskers, teleport home when stuck; recast as the escape hatch |
| Co-op painted into a corner | Low / High | Intent commands, per-entity hit-stop, tokens per player, a list of anchors, no `localPlayer`, the architecture test |
| An asset pipeline step strips `socket_staff` | Med / High | `optimize-assets` skips the player; `check-assets` asserts the socket |
| A generated file drifts from the art | Med / Med | `gen:moves -- --check` in the checks; never edited by hand |
| Mixed sibling art styles | Med / Low | One lighting and fog setup, a colour grade; final art later |
| Weaknesses feel mandatory or frustrating | Med / Med | Nothing is immune (×0.5 still works); mixed camps; icons revealed on the first hit; first-time toasts; the paired bot check |
| Scope creep | High / Med | The non-goals; M7 runs only after M5; `docs/plans/backlog.md` |

## 11. Explicit non-goals for this slice

- No networking, co-op or server sim.
- No second area.
- No final art, no new Higgsfield generations, no enemy melee clips.
- No hub integration. A seam is left in `app/` **(architect's call)**.
- No crafting, affixes, skill trees, mounts, swimming, climbing, day/night, weather, cutscenes,
  voice, localization (strings are still in data), touch controls, character creator or NG+.
- No navmesh. No WebGPU: we stay on WebGL2 **(architect's call)**.
- No difficulty settings or modes, ever in the slice (owner's answer 3).
- No multiple save slots.
